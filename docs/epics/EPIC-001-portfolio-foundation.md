# EPIC-001 — Portfolio foundation and professional narrative

## Outcome

A single, editable source of truth presents Fabian Fernández as a Senior Software Engineer and GenAI builder without unsupported claims.

## Context

The supplied CV establishes React/Next.js, TypeScript, cloud, AI-powered systems, and recent multi-agent human-in-the-loop work as the professional core.

## Scope

- Define identity, positioning, bio, capabilities, stack, experience, projects, and contact fields.
- Separate verified CV facts from placeholders that require human input.
- Establish Spanish-first copy that can be localized later.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every public professional claim is traceable to the CV or explicitly marked for human completion.
2. No employer, date, metric, email, or social URL is invented.
3. Content can be updated without changing page layout logic.
4. The featured case study explains the agentic AI harness and human-in-the-loop value.

## Non-goals

- Writing a blog or CMS.
- Publishing confidential employer or client information.

## Dependencies

None.
