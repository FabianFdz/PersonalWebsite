# EPIC-002 — Editorial portfolio experience

## Outcome

A distinctive, fast, responsive one-page portfolio inspired by the information rhythm of tanvir.io while retaining an original visual identity.

## Scope

- Hero, experience, featured project, capabilities, stack, and contact sections.
- Editorial typography, strong grid, restrained palette, and intentional accent color.
- Keyboard, touch, mobile, tablet, and large-screen behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The first viewport identifies Fabian and his specialization immediately.
2. All sections remain legible at 320px width without horizontal page overflow.
3. Navigation uses semantic anchors and works with keyboard input.
4. The design is recognizably original and does not reuse tanvir.io assets or copy.
5. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.

## Non-goals

- Reproducing another portfolio pixel for pixel.
- Complex animation that delays or obscures content.

- EPIC-001.
